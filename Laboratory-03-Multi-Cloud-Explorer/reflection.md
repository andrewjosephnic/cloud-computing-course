# Mission Reflection

## Reflection

The cloud platform that impressed me the most is AWS because of its wide range of services and its ability to support different types of workloads. While researching AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, I learned that each platform has different strengths and can be suitable for different organizations and business requirements.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, identity management, databases, security, and other cloud services. They also allow organizations to scale resources according to their needs instead of depending entirely on physical infrastructure.

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud platform because different organizations have different priorities. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit more from Azure, while an organization focused on artificial intelligence and Kubernetes may consider Google Cloud. AWS can be a good choice when an organization needs a broad selection of cloud services and scalable infrastructure.

As a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. The decision should consider the organization's workload, existing technologies, budget, scalability requirements, security needs, and future goals.

My GitHub portfolio also improved through this activity because I learned how to organize cloud research and documentation into separate Markdown files. I also learned how to compare cloud providers, recommend platforms for different clients, and document my work using GitHub. This activity helped me understand how cloud knowledge can be applied to real business scenarios.
